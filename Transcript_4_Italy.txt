Expert 4 – Dr. Marco Rossi
Role: Chief of Colorectal Surgery
Market: Italy

00:00
Interviewer: Thank you for speaking with us today. How is robotic surgery adoption progressing across Italian healthcare institutions?

00:22
Dr. Rossi: In Italy, adoption has followed a distinct regional divide. Northern regions like Lombardy and Veneto have heavily invested through regional healthcare funds, whereas southern public hospitals face severe fiscal deficits and rely on second-hand or leased robotic systems.

01:15
Interviewer: What is the primary purchasing constraint for Italian hospitals?

01:28
Dr. Rossi: The biggest constraint is the Consip national procurement framework. Public tenders take between 14 to 20 months to clear regulatory hurdles, and regional healthcare quotas strictly limit how many high-cost robotic platforms can be acquired per province.

02:30
Interviewer: What about robotic procedures outside of urology?

02:45
Dr. Rossi: We are seeing rapid growth in complex colorectal and bariatric procedures. Multi-specialty utilization is mandatory for Italian hospitals to approve financing; a robot dedicated exclusively to urology will almost certainly be rejected by administrative directors.

03:50
Interviewer: What is your 3 to 5 year outlook for the Italian market?

04:05
Dr. Rossi: We project around 25 percent annual procedure expansion in certified regional cancer hubs, but widespread adoption in community facilities will depend entirely on lower-cost modular robotic entrants entering the Italian market.
